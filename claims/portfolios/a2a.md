# Finance-to-Legal handoff

The connected showcase runs `legal-agent` on its private Docker network. The Finance service reads its authenticated Agent Card at `/.well-known/agent-card.json`, checks the pinned endpoint and skill, and calls `message/send` over HTTP. The local peer implements the A2A 0.3 JSON-RPC task exchange for the synthetic `legal.expense-authorization` skill. [A2A 0.3 specification](https://a2a-protocol.org/v0.3.0/specification/)

Finance approves a single inquiry for FIN-207. Its structured data contains the finance and matter IDs, invoice and expense references, and the amount in cents. The Legal agent acknowledges a working task. The Workbench delivers the obligation to the assigned Legal employee after that acknowledgment. The reviewer can approve a shareable disposition and an authorization-register reference. The service sends that decision as a continuation using the separate Legal credential. Finance then reads the completed task with `tasks/get` and checks the task, context, artifact and approved result before updating its mailbox.

PostgreSQL stores the employee actions and pending deliveries. The Legal peer keeps accepted messages and results in SQLite on its own named volume. The worker retries pending deliveries after a restart or missed acknowledgment using the original message ID. Local service credentials and private HTTP illustrate this bilateral agreement. The showcase's peer has no public host port, live vendor integration, or connection to an accounting system. The internal credential pair and reference allowlist apply to this synthetic fixture.
